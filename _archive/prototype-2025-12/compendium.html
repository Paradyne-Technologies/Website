The Knowledge Acquisition Problem

Every federated simulation project faces the same challenge: where does the data come from? Programs spend months compiling entity specifications from fragmented sources—subject matter experts, outdated databases, proprietary spreadsheets, and conflicting documentation.

The result? Inconsistent data across federates, interoperability failures during exercises, and audit findings that trace back to undocumented source data. It's a problem that costs millions and delays programs by months.

The Compendium Solution

Compendium eliminates the Knowledge Acquisition bottleneck. Instead of months spent compiling data from scratch, you start with a comprehensive, authoritative dataset that's already tied to official SISO DIS Enumerations.

Every record includes full provenance documentation—you know exactly where the data came from and can demonstrate compliance during audits. It's the foundational data layer that the entire defense simulation industry can depend on.
