About Paradyne

Building the foundational data layer that the entire defense simulation industry can depend on.

Our Mission

Paradyne Technologies provides authoritative specification data infrastructure for defense simulation and training systems. We exist to solve a fundamental problem: the defense simulation industry lacks a reliable, consistent foundation for entity data.

Every federated simulation project reinvents the wheel—spending months compiling data from fragmented sources, subject matter experts, and outdated databases. The result is inconsistent datasets, interoperability failures, and audit risks. We're building the infrastructure to change that.

Precision. Authority. Trust.

The foundation for simulation data.

Our Story

Paradyne was founded by three partners who spent years working at the intersection of software development, data architecture, and defense simulation systems. We saw the same problem repeated across every program we touched: teams spending months on Knowledge Acquisition, piecing together entity specifications from unreliable sources, and then struggling with interoperability when those datasets didn't align.

We knew there had to be a better way. The simulation industry needed authoritative, well-documented data infrastructure—a single source of truth that programs could depend on instead of rebuilding from scratch.

That's what we're building. Paradyne is a small, focused team creating foundational infrastructure for the industry. We combine deep domain expertise with modern software engineering to deliver data products that meet the rigorous standards defense programs require.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we build.

Data Integrity

Every record we publish is traceable to its source. We maintain rigorous documentation standards because accuracy isn't optional in defense simulation—it's the foundation everything else depends on.

Domain Expertise

We understand the simulation domain deeply—the standards, the workflows, the real-world constraints. Our products are built by people who have lived the problems we're solving.

Operational Excellence

We build infrastructure that works reliably, day after day. Our systems are designed for the demands of enterprise defense programs—because downtime and errors have real consequences.

Customer Focus

We succeed when our customers succeed. We listen carefully, respond quickly, and build the features and capabilities that matter most to the people using our products.

Continuous Improvement

The simulation domain evolves constantly—new platforms, new standards, new requirements. We're committed to keeping pace, continuously expanding and refining our data and capabilities.

Our Approach

Small Team, Big Impact

We're intentionally small and focused. Every person on our team brings deep expertise and direct ownership of their work. No bureaucracy, no handoffs—just people who care deeply about getting it right.

Standards-First

We build on SISO standards because interoperability matters. Our data is designed to work seamlessly across federated environments, not lock you into proprietary formats.

Long-Term Partnership

We're not building quick fixes—we're building infrastructure for the long haul. Our goal is to be a trusted partner that programs can depend on across the full lifecycle of their systems.
