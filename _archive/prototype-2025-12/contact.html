Frequently Asked Questions

How does the pilot program work?

Our 90-day pilot gives you full access to Compendium to evaluate with your team. We provide dedicated onboarding, help you integrate with your existing workflows, and offer direct access to our engineering team for technical questions. At the end of the pilot, you'll have a clear picture of how Paradyne fits your program's needs.

What data formats do you support?

Compendium data is accessible via RESTful API and can be exported in multiple formats including JSON and XML. We also provide documentation and sample code to help integrate with common simulation frameworks.

Is DE Dictionary really free?

Yes, DE Dictionary is available as a free download on iOS and Android. It's our way of giving back to the simulation community and helping professionals who work with DIS protocols every day.

How is your data sourced and validated?

All Compendium data is tied to official sources with full provenance documentation. Our data team combines automated validation with domain expert review to ensure accuracy. Every record includes source attribution so you can verify and demonstrate compliance during audits.
